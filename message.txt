__**Current global perks :**__

**Balloons**

> Balloons are basically global loot boxes.
> 
> When someone buys a Balloon Drop, **every player gets 1 balloon** with a rarity ranging from **Uncommon** to **Legendary**.
> 
> Players who are offline at the time of purchase can still claim their balloon by logging in within **3 days**.

**Blessings**

> Blessings are positive effects selected from a pool of helpful buffs.
> 
> When someone buys a Blessing, the effect is applied to **every player online** for the duration of the blessing.
